The Best Movies According to Furtuna

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Dirty Dancing

Romantic drama of a young women discovering confidence, independence, and love through dance while challenging the class divisions, expectations, and social rules of her sheltered world.

Trading Places

A comedy and clever story about wealth, privilege, and how circumstances can shape people’s lives.

Hidden Figures

Three Black women at NASA overcome racial and gender discrimination while making major contributions to the U.S. space program.
